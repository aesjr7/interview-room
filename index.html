Answer structures

Pick a structure in two seconds and the answer organizes itself.

STARSituation · Task · Action · Result. For "Tell me about a time...". One or two lines of context, action with "I" (not only "we"), result with a number.
Past · Present · FutureFor "Tell me about yourself". Where you came from, what you do now, where you are heading.
PREPPoint · Reason · Example · Point. For opinion questions. Position, reason, example, restate the position.
Rule of three"Three things..." organizes any answer and signals executive clarity.

Golden rules

  • Never speak badly of past jobs, bosses or companies. Never.
  • Salary: only after a signal of an offer. Ready exit:
  • Always have 3 questions prepared for the end. Leaving with none kills the role.
  • Twenty minutes of research before each interview: product, revenue model, news from the last 6 months, competitors, the interviewers' LinkedIn.
  • Spoken answers of 60 to 90 seconds. Past two minutes, you have lost the room.
  • End every answer on your strongest point, not on "so... yeah".
